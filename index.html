About

I'm Haris Ali, a graphic designer and print specialist from Pakistan, working from Deira, Dubai with clients across the UAE. For seven years I've turned complex briefs into clear visuals: logos, marketing collateral, and the websites that carry a brand online.

I care about the last step as much as the first. A poster is only finished when it prints cleanly, so I prepare files with the press in mind, and I build sites so the brand looks the same on a screen as on paper.
